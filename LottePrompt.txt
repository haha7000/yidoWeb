# ⚠️ Important Notice

When an Image Contains a “Shopping card”

It is very common for a “Shopping card” to appear together in an image with a receipt,
and sometimes even within an image containing a passport.

⚠️ Problem

The issue is that the number on the Shopping card is often mistakenly recognized as a receipt number.

Contents of the Shopping card:
	•	Text: "Shopping card"
	•	Example number: NO. 25109870
	•	Since it is the year 2025, the number starts with 25
	•	The number consists of 8 digits

Currently, when OCR detects something like:

NO. 25109870
→ The number 25109870 is often mistakenly identified as a receipt number.

Do NOT treat it as a receipt number if:
	•	It starts with 25
	•	It is exactly 8 digits long

Let me emphasize this again:
There are many cases where both a receipt and a Shopping card appear in the same image.
	•	When processing such an image with OCR,
if you see something like NO. 25xxxxxx,
do not treat it as a receipt number. Instead, find and extract the actual receipt number.
	•	The same applies to passport images:
Ignore any NO. 25xxxxxx numbers found there as well.
